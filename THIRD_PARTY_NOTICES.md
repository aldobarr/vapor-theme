# Third-party notices

Vapor combines a small, desktop-only subset of visual material from the
following pinned upstream projects. Exact revisions and per-file SHA-256
digests are recorded in `upstream/pins.json` and in every release's
`provenance.json`.

## Bazzite

Source: <https://github.com/ublue-os/bazzite>

The Bazzite logo, Convergence wallpaper, adaptive-transparency configuration,
and desktop-specific patches are redistributed from the pinned Bazzite
revision. Bazzite's repository license is Apache-2.0; its
`steamdeck-kde-presets` packaging also carries a GPL-2.0 license file. Both
license texts are included in generated Vapor releases.

Apache-2.0 does not grant trademark rights. The Bazzite name and logo remain
the property of their respective owner. This independent Vapor packaging is
not endorsed by or affiliated with Bazzite or Universal Blue. Redistributors
are responsible for confirming that their use of Bazzite branding is
permitted.

## Valve SteamOS KDE presets

Source: <https://gitlab.com/evlaV/steamdeck-kde-presets>

The Vapor color scheme, Plasma Style, Global Theme structure, splash artwork,
preview, busy indicator, and Plasma setup scripts originate in the SteamOS KDE
presets snapshot identified by `upstream/pins.json`. Bazzite's GPL-2.0
packaging license text is included with generated releases; it must not be
read as a grant of trademark rights in Valve, Steam, or SteamOS branding.

Vapor excludes Steam Deck icons, Gaming Mode assets, VGUI/GTK/Konsole themes,
Steam helpers, and game artwork.
